Same records. Ten settled sales. Two counting rules. A 1000 bps gap.

DisputeRadar shows how the counting rule changes the ratio a merchant team reviews. Under fictional rule 1 every dispute and fraud event reported in October counts; under fictional rule 2 each transaction with such an event counts once. Same records, same month, different number. A person reviews the result. The tool makes no determination.

What this is not

Not a real rule
DEMO-EVENT-COUNT and its versions are fictional. They are not Visa VAMP, not Mastercard and not acquirer or processor policy. The 2500 bps line is illustrative.
Not a decision
Results are for human review. No fine, monitoring-programme or recovery prediction, no risk rating and no compliance determination.
Zero is not all-clear
With no settled sales in the month the ratio is undefined, not zero. Fictional merchant M2 shows this in the workbench.
No network connection
The workbench runs in your browser and sends nothing. There is no processor feed and no paid service.
Imports are unverified
Anything you paste or import is labelled UNVERIFIED USER-SUPPLIED. The app cannot detect or prevent real or sensitive data, so do not paste real cardholder, personal or confidential data.
